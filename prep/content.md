# Content
Topic: Forensics

## Overview/Introduction/Context
* Crime scene investigators use technology to collect, examine, and analyze evidence.
* Software helps investigators analyze DNA, compare fingerprints, examine digital evidence, and organize case information.


## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
1. Codis
  * CODIS compares DNA profiles to help investigators find possible matches between crime scene evidence and DNA databases.
  * https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/dna-fingerprint-act-of-2005-expungement-policy/codis-and-ndis-fact-sheet

2. Next Generation Identification (NGI)
* NGI helps law enforcement search fingerprint records and other biometric information to identify people.
* https://le.fbi.gov/science-and-lab/biometrics-and-fingerprints/biometrics/next-generation-identification-ngi
  
3. OSIRIS
* OSIRIS is software that helps forensic laboratories review and analyze DNA data.
  
4. GeneMapper ID
* GeneMapper ID helps forensic scientists analyze DNA markers and genetic profiles.

6. GeneMapper ID-X
* GeneMapper ID-X helps forensic laboratories examine DNA analysis results.

7. TrueAllele
* TrueAllele uses statistical methods to help forensic scientists analyze complex DNA mixtures.
  
8. Autopsy
* Autopsy helps investigators examine computer files and other digital evidence.
  
9. EnCase Forensic
* EnCase Forensic helps investigators examine and report digital evidence from electronic devices.

10. Magnet AXIOM
* Magnet AXIOM helps investigators examine digital evidence from computers and mobile devices.

11. Cellebrite
* tools help forensic professionals examine digital information from electronic devices.

12. X-Ways Forensics
* X-Ways Forensics helps investigators examine storage devices, search files, and organize digital evidence.

13. PhotoRec
* PhotoRec helps recover certain lost or deleted files from storage devices.
  
14. NIST Computer Forensics Tool Catalog
* This catalog provides information about tools used to examine digital evidence.

15. Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)
* LIMS helps forensic laboratories track evidence samples, test results, and laboratory records.


##### Hardware
1. Digital Forensics Workstation:
   * a powerful computer used by forensics investigators to examine digital evidence, analyze files, and orangize investigation results.
     * https://www.nist.gov
2. Forensics Camera
  * used to photograph crime scenes and record evidence before anything is moved. Professional cameras can cost hundreds of thousands of dollars
3. Fingerprint scanner
* captures fingerprints electronically so they can be examined and compared
4. Alternate light source
* uses different types of light to help investigators locate certain types of evidence that may not be easily visible
5. Forensics comparison microscope
* Allows scientist to examine two samples side by side, such as fibers, hairs, or other materials
6. Digital evidence imaging device
* used to create copies of data from digital storage devices for forensics examination
7. Forensics light microscope
* helps forensics scientist examine small pieces of evidence that cannot be easily seen.
8. 3D crime scene scanner
* creates a three-dimensional digital model of a crime scene so investigators can review its layout later
9. Evidence drying cabinet
* used to dry certain collected evidence under controlled conditions before it is packaged and stored
10. digital caliper
* measures small objects and evidence accurately, including dimensions of physical items
11. forensics x-ray machine
* allows investigators to examine the inside of certain objects without opening or damaging them
12. portable evidence scanner
* used to scan documents, photographs, and other paper evidence into digital files
13. Gps device
* records location information and can help investigators document where evidence or important locations are found
14. forensics evidence refrigerator
* keeps certain biological evidence in controlled, cold storage to help preserve it until examination
15. portable digital recorder
* records interviews, statements, and other audio information that may be relevant to an investigation
